Privacy Policy

Robot Rescue Island — Rao Ventures LLC

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Robot Rescue Island is a game for players ages 9 and above and their families, offered for U.S. distribution. Rao Ventures LLC publishes and operates the game and handles privacy requests at the contact details below. This policy explains what information is used, why it is used, and the choices available to players and parents.

Playing the game

Robot Rescue Island includes River Rescue and an optional daily movement break. Children can play without creating an account. There are no advertisements, in-app purchases, chat rooms, public profiles, or public leaderboards.

The game does not ask for a child’s date of birth, email address, location, photos, or recordings from the camera or microphone. Please use a made-up nickname rather than a child’s real name.

Information saved on your device

Your nickname, points, badges, completed river crossings, levels, and daily progress are saved on your device. We do not provide an online game account or a service that synchronizes these saves between devices.

Your device provider may include game saves in backups, depending on your settings. Removing the game or clearing its data may remove local progress. Restoring a backup may restore that progress.

No in-game tracking or recording

This release does not send your nickname, game progress, screen visits, taps, or solution results to analytics or recording services. We do not record play sessions or send in-game crash reports to those services.

There is no in-game advertising or tracking for targeted advertising. Children do not need to send us personal information to earn points, receive badges, or access the game.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. This release does not track players across other companies’ apps or websites, or enable third parties to do so through the game. There is no such tracking to turn off in response to a Do Not Track signal.

Understanding how the game is used

We may look at summary reports made available by Apple and Google, such as total downloads and general usage or reliability statistics. These reports are provided through the stores, under their own privacy policies and sharing settings. The game does not send additional usage information to us to create these reports.

Children and parents

Robot Rescue Island is directed to players ages 9 and above, including children under 13. We keep game progress on the device and do not require children to send us personal information to play.

Parents can help a child change or clear their nickname in Profile. They can remove local game data using the device’s settings, subject to backup and restore behavior. We cannot view, edit, or erase saves stored only on your device.

A parent may contact us to review or delete their child’s personal information that we hold, and refuse further collection or use. We cannot retrieve saves that remain only on your device. Please do not send a child’s identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

Contacting support

A parent or guardian can email us for help. We receive the sender’s email address, message, and any attachments they choose to send. Our email provider processes those messages so we can respond. Please avoid sending a child’s full name, photographs, or other unnecessary personal information.

If a child sends a one-time question, we use only the contact information needed to answer that question. We do not use it to contact the child again, market to them, or build a profile. We delete unnecessary child information and attachments without using them. Ongoing help should come from a parent or guardian.

How long information is kept

Game saves remain on your device until they are removed, subject to your backup settings. We do not receive those saves.

Parent or adult support messages are used to resolve the request and allow follow-up about that same issue. Our retention rule is to delete them within 30 days after resolution, or sooner when they are no longer needed. You can request earlier deletion.

A child’s one-time question and contact details are used only for the response and are deleted promptly after responding, no later than 24 hours afterward. Unnecessary child information is deleted promptly upon discovery. We do not keep children’s support information for analytics or product improvement.

If a specific law requires a record to be retained, we keep only the required information for that legal purpose and delete it when the requirement ends. These timeframes are our retention rules, not a statement that the law requires a 30-day or 24-hour period.

Earlier test versions

Earlier test versions used Vexo and Expo for testing with adults only. Those versions may have sent information about play and errors, including device codes and screen recordings. This release stops that collection. Stopping collection does not automatically remove previously sent information. Adult testers can contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of their test records.

When information may be shared

We do not send this release’s gameplay records to advertising or analytics companies. Our email service provider handles support messages to deliver and store correspondence so we can answer. We require providers handling personal information on our behalf to limit its use to the agreed service and provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable requirements. Our service providers may process support information outside the United States.

We may disclose the minimum information necessary to comply with a legal requirement or protect people, security, or legal rights, as permitted by law.

Review, correction, deletion, and privacy complaints

Players, adult testers, and parents or guardians acting for a child can email support@raoventures.co to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or to raise a privacy complaint. Describe the request and provide only enough information to locate the correspondence or record. We may take reasonable steps to verify identity or parental authority before disclosing or changing information.

We will address requests within the time required by applicable law. If a request cannot be fulfilled, we will explain the reason and any available next steps. Exercising privacy rights does not affect access to the game. Clearing a local nickname or save is done on your device; removing a store account is handled by Apple or Google.

Apple App Store and Google Play

Apple and Google handle store accounts, downloads, and store transactions under their own terms and privacy policies. The game does not ask for your store password or payment-card information. Requests about a store account should be made to the relevant store; deleting game progress does not delete that account.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Keeping information safe and updating this policy

The game reduces exposure by keeping progress in local app storage and disabling in-game analytics and session recording. Local saves are not a secure vault for sensitive information. Your device’s security and backup settings also affect their protection.

Our support-handling rules limit access to people who need it to answer requests, require protected accounts and secure connections when accessing correspondence, and require deletion under the retention rules above. No way of storing or sending information is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive information in a nickname or support message.

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the latest revision date above. Any future plan to collect children’s personal information will be reviewed before it starts. We will provide any notice and obtain any parental consent required by law; agreeing to the game terms alone does not give that consent.
