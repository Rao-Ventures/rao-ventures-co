My Ashtanga Yoga Practice

Authentic Sanskrit Audio & Guided Ashtanga Yoga Primary Series

My Ashtanga Yoga Practice is an interactive mobile application crafted for practitioners learning and mastering the traditional Ashtanga Yoga Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa).

Deepen your Ashtanga Yoga self-practice with immersive audio-guided sequences, customizable breath pacing tailored to your natural rhythm, and authentic opening and closing chants. It's designed to adapt to your practice level whether you're a beginner or an experienced practitioner.

Core Features

  • 🧘
    Primary Series & Custom Sequences

    Practice Sun Salutations A & B, Quarter Primary, Half Primary, Full Primary (Yoga Chikitsa), or the Finishing Sequence.

  • 🕉️
    Sanskrit Asana Library & Pronunciations

    Searchable pose directory with accurate Sanskrit pronunciations, Devanagari script, English translations, and drishti gazing points.

  • 🔢
    Traditional Sanskrit Vinyasa Counts

    Authentic Sanskrit counting synchronized with inhalations and exhalations for a mindful practice.

  • ⏱️
    Customize Audio Guidance & Breath Pacing

    Configure your practice in Settings: adjust breath cadence (from 4 to 14 Breaths Per Minute, default 9 BPM), toggle Sanskrit counts, Sun Salutations reps (3 vs 5), and intermediate vinyasa flows.

  • 🎶
    Opening & Closing Chants

    Line-by-line audio chanting with original Sanskrit text, Romanized transliteration, and English translations.

  • 💓
    Apple Health & Google Health Connect

    Automatically log mindful minutes, active calories, and heart rate directly on your device with zero cloud tracking.

Svastha

Ancient Wisdom for Modern Life

Svastha is your companion for exploring Hindu scriptures, deepening spiritual practice, and building consistent meditation habits. Study the Upanishads with AI-powered guidance that brings ancient texts to life in your daily routine.

Built with care by Rao Ventures, Svastha bridges the gap between millennia-old spiritual wisdom and modern tools—offering a seamless, personalized experience that grows with your practice.

Key Features

  • 📖
    Scripture Library

    7+ Upanishads with verse-by-verse study, bookmarks, and journaling

  • 🤖
    AI Scripture Guide

    RAG-powered AI that provides grounded, contextual answers from the texts

  • 📊
    Reading Progress

    Track your journey through each scripture and stay motivated as you advance

  • 📓
    Journaling

    Reflect on verses and capture personal insights with integrated journaling

Flow Into Stillness

Your Sanctuary for Inner Peace

Flow Into Stillness is a wellness platform dedicated to making yoga accessible, informative, and easy to incorporate into daily life. It combines a rich yoga pose library with clear guidance on instructions, benefits, contraindications, and modifications.

Whether you're a beginner taking your first steps on the mat or an experienced practitioner deepening your practice, Flow Into Stillness provides the tools you need to grow safely and confidently.

Key Features

  • 🧘
    Pose Library

    70+ yoga poses featuring detailed instructions, benefits, and contraindications, with new poses added weekly

  • 💡
    Inspiration Hub

    Motivation, wisdom quotes, and insights for your spiritual journey

  • 🛡️
    Safe Practice

    Modifications and contraindications for practicing safely and confidently
