Privacy Policy

My Ashtanga Yoga Practice — Rao Ventures LLC

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

The My Ashtanga Yoga Practice mobile application (the "App") is published and operated by Rao Ventures LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data You Provide

We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.

B. App Usage & Local Data Storage

Your practice preferences and workout records are stored locally on your device. We also collect pseudonymous usage and diagnostic information to improve the App, as described below.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscription Processing

When you purchase a subscription or unlock premium features, transactions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We work with a secure subscription management service (RevenueCat) to verify subscription status, validate purchase receipts, and restore purchases.

  • We do not collect, receive, or store your credit card details or financial information; all payments are handled directly by Apple or Google.
  • A pseudonymous, randomly generated identifier is used to associate and verify your active subscription status.

We retain subscription-related information for as long as necessary to verify purchases, manage subscription access, restore purchases, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting support@raoventures.co.

D. Diagnostic & Usage Information

We use third-party analytics providers to process pseudonymous usage and diagnostic information to improve app features, performance, and reliability. This includes app interactions, feature usage, device and app information, and error or performance reports.

Usage information is associated with a pseudonymous device identifier and app sessions. We analyze this information to understand usage trends, improve features, and maintain app reliability.

Individual usage and diagnostic records are retained for 60 days. You may request deletion by contacting support@raoventures.co.

Apple and Google may also collect crash and performance reports in accordance with your device privacy settings.

E. Apple Health (iOS)

On iOS, the App offers an optional feature to record completed practice sessions (such as start time, duration, and workout type) to Apple Health. This data:

  • Is recorded only after you grant explicit permission through the system permission prompt.
  • Is stored locally in Apple Health on your device; we do not transmit, receive, or store your health data on our servers.
  • Is never used for advertising, marketing, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than logging your practice in Apple Health.
  • Can be managed or revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → My Ashtanga Yoga Practice.

F. Google Health Connect (Android)

On Android, the App offers an optional feature to record completed practice sessions (such as start time, duration, and exercise type) to Google Health Connect. This data:

  • Is recorded only after you grant explicit permission through the Health Connect permission prompt.
  • Is stored locally in Health Connect on your device; we do not transmit, receive, or store your health data on our servers.
  • Is never used for advertising, marketing, shared with data brokers, or used for any purpose other than recording your practice session to Google Health Connect on your device.
  • Complies strictly with the Google Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Can be managed or revoked at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → My Ashtanga Practice.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Save your practice settings and preferences locally on your device.
  • Deliver and validate your active Pro subscription access.
  • Monitor app stability, performance, and reliability across supported platforms.
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve app performance and usability.
  • Record completed practice sessions to Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) as Yoga workouts, solely with your explicit permission.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We use third-party providers, including RevenueCat and Vexo, to support subscriptions, analytics, and app reliability. These providers process relevant information to deliver their services. Health records are handled through Apple Health or Google Health Connect as described above. We do not sell personal data.

We are based in the United States, and our service providers process information in the United States. If you use the App from another country, your information may be processed outside that country, where data protection laws may differ. Contact support@raoventures.co for information about this processing.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security of Your Data

Your practice preferences are stored locally on your device. You can remove local app data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through your device settings. Health records can be managed separately in Apple Health or Google Health Connect.

Removing the App does not automatically delete information already held by our service providers. Requests to delete subscription information do not cancel subscriptions; manage cancellations through Apple or Google.

6. Your Privacy Rights

A. Responsibility & Subscription Processing

Rao Ventures LLC is the controller responsible for the personal information processed for the purposes described in this policy. Our contact details appear below.

We process subscription-related information to perform our contract with you by verifying purchases, providing subscription access, and restoring purchases. This information comes from your App installation and purchase information supplied through Apple or Google and our subscription provider. Without the information needed to verify a purchase, we may be unable to provide or restore paid access.

B. Requests & Choices

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, restriction of its use, and a portable copy of information you have provided.

Right to object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Permissions for the optional health features can be withdrawn in your device settings as described above.

Send privacy requests to support@raoventures.co. Because the App does not require an account, we may need limited additional information to identify the relevant app installation or purchase records and verify your request. We respond within the periods required by applicable law and explain any permitted extension or reason we cannot fulfill a request.

Where applicable law provides this right, you may also complain to the relevant privacy or data protection authority. You do not have to contact us first unless applicable law requires it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App's legal documentation and in our App Store / Google Play listings.
