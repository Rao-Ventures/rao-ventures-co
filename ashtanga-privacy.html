Privacy Policy

My Ashtanga Yoga Practice — Rao Ventures LLC

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

The My Ashtanga Yoga Practice mobile application (the "App") is published and operated by Rao Ventures LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data You Provide

We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.

B. App Usage & Local Data Storage

All your practice settings (such as breath timing preferences, audio settings, and progress data) are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal practice data on external servers.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscription Processing

When you purchase a subscription or unlock premium features, transactions are processed securely through the Apple App Store (via Apple In-App Purchase) or Google Play Store (via Google Play Billing). We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscription status, validate purchase receipts, and restore purchases.

  • We do not collect, receive, or store your credit card details or financial information.
  • RevenueCat uses an anonymous, random App User ID to associate and verify your active subscription entitlement without linking it to your personal identity.

D. Automatically Collected Information & Telemetry

To improve app quality, performance, stability, and user experience, the App uses trusted developer telemetry and analytics services to collect aggregate, non-personally identifiable technical and usage data:

  • EAS Insights (Expo Application Services): Collects aggregate operational and diagnostic metrics, such as app launch/cold start events, app store version distribution, and platform performance trends.
  • Vexo Analytics (vexo.co): Collects aggregate usage events (such as screen navigation, feature interactions, device model, and OS version).

Neither service collects your name, email, physical location, audio recordings, or private practice data. Standard non-identifying crash and performance reports may also be collected by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) in accordance with your device privacy settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Save your custom breath rate (Breaths Per Minute) and practice preferences locally on your device.
  • Deliver and validate your active Pro subscription access via RevenueCat.
  • Monitor app stability, launch reliability, and version adoption across platforms via EAS Insights.
  • Analyze aggregated app usage patterns and navigation trends to optimize app performance and usability via Vexo Analytics.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal user data with third-party advertisers. Diagnostic telemetry and subscription validation are processed strictly through Expo (EAS Insights), RevenueCat, and Vexo Analytics for app stability, subscription fulfillment, functionality, and user experience improvement. No third-party targeted advertising networks, marketing trackers, or tracking pixels are integrated into the App.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security of Your Data

Your data security is preserved by storing all user preferences locally on your physical device. You can clear all app data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through your device settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App's legal documentation and in our App Store / Google Play listings.
