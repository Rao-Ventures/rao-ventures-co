1. Information Handled & Why

Practice data on your device

Your settings, practice history, and first-open date are stored on your device. They support your preferences, Progress calendar, practice log, and seven-day preview. The App does not upload these records to our servers. Optional health recording is separate.

Purchases and access

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payments. Our subscription-management provider processes a randomly generated customer identifier, purchase receipts or references, subscription status, and technical information needed to verify and restore access. Subscription checks can occur even if you have not purchased. We do not receive your payment-card details; purchase and subscription records are still information we process.

Online voice guidance

Our speech-generation provider receives standard yoga instruction text and connection information, such as your IP address, to generate requested audio. The App does not send your workout records for this purpose.

Bundled text, timers, chants, and recorded counts work offline. Online speech requires Internet unless audio is cached in the current session; the cache is not a permanent download library. If online speech fails, the App attempts device speech, whose availability depends on installed voices and device settings. Full voice guidance is not guaranteed offline.

Optional health recording

With system permission, the App writes completed yoga sessions, including start/end times, duration, and exercise type, to Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. It does not read your existing health records or upload these workouts to our servers. We do not use these records for advertising or sell or share them with data brokers.

Manage records and revoke permission in your device's Apple Health or Health Connect settings. The platform's own storage, backup, and synchronization settings apply. Our use of Health Connect information is limited to this user-facing workout-recording function and follows its applicable permissions and Limited Use requirements.

Support, updates, and platform services

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and information you choose to send. The feedback draft includes app version, platform, and your customer identifier when available; you can review it before sending. We use email and support-service providers to receive correspondence and resolve requests. Do not send health records, passwords, or payment-card details.

The App contacts an app-update delivery service to check for and deliver updates. This involves connection information and app/device information needed for update delivery. Apple, Google, and device speech services may also process technical information under their own settings and policies. The App does not send interaction events to a usage-analytics provider or use a third-party crash-reporting service. Store diagnostic reports depend on your platform privacy settings.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information we handle. Contact us to request deletion of information held by us or by providers acting on our behalf, subject to applicable rights and lawful retention requirements. Uninstalling or updating the App does not automatically erase provider records or information collected by earlier versions. Deleting subscription records does not cancel renewal; cancel through your purchasing store.
